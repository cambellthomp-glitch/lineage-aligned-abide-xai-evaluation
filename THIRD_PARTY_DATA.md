# Third-party data

ABIDE-I and ABIDE-II raw imaging and phenotypic data are third-party resources available from the official access pages: https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/abide/abide_I.html and https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/abide/abide_II.html . Preprocessed derivatives are described at https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/abide/preprocessed.html and through the Preprocessed Connectomes Project ABIDE resource at https://preprocessed-connectomes-project.github.io/abide/ . The external branch used the public ABIDE-fMRIPrep 25.2.4 derivative project. Provider registration, acknowledgment, and data-use requirements apply.

The author does not own and cannot relicense ABIDE-I/II source data or participant-level preprocessed derivatives. None is redistributed here. Neither the MIT License nor CC BY 4.0 applies to those third-party resources. Readers must reacquire the data from official channels. This package also excludes subject identifiers, mappings, labels, OOF records, per-subject attributions, participant-level probability data, and the unclear-ownership `cc200_yeo7_mapping.csv` support file.
